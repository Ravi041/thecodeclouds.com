# Editorial cover artwork

Created 2026-09-05 using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, not the fallback CLI. These are original illustrative assets, not evidence of a real event or the author's own workspace. No Techbeatly artwork, logos, course discounts or event claims were copied. Final files are locally served JPEGs, mechanically resized to 1200×675 and compressed at quality 85; the generated compositions are otherwise unchanged.

- `src/assets/images/kubernetes-troubleshooting.jpg` — Kubernetes article, homepage feature and cards; 118,520 bytes.
- `src/assets/images/project-learning.jpg` — project-led learning article and cards; 111,430 bytes.

Both articles have descriptive alternative text, image-specific social metadata, and visible AI-generation credits below the cover. Text-only covers remain available for other articles.
